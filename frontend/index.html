      1
      🔑 Get Gemini API Key (Free)
      Go to aistudio.google.com/app/apikey → Sign in → Create API Key → Copy it. Then add to backend/.env as GEMINI_API_KEY=AIza... and restart the server.
      2
      👤 Set Your Profile
      Go to Settings → Your Profile. Fill in your name, domain (e.g. "Full Stack Developer"), and skills. This makes all AI outputs personalized to YOU — not generic.
      3
      📄 Upload Your Resume
      Go to Settings → Resume Upload. Upload your PDF resume. BRO-BOT will extract the text and use it to write more accurate job application messages and personalized posts.
      4
      ✨ Create a Post
      Go to Content Studio. Pick a template or write a topic. Select platform (LinkedIn/Instagram/Twitter). Pick tone. Click Generate with AI. Edit if needed. Save to Queue.
      5
      📋 Approve Posts
      Go to Post Queue. All generated posts sit here waiting for your approval. Click ✅ Approve to mark them ready. Then manually copy-paste to LinkedIn/Instagram (Phase 2 will auto-post).
      6
      💼 Hunt for Jobs
      Go to Job Hunter. Type a keyword and location, click Scan Jobs Now. BRO-BOT will scrape Indeed, Naukri, and Internshala and show results in ~60 seconds. Click ✅ Applied when you apply.
      7
      💬 Handle Recruiter Messages
      Got a LinkedIn message from a recruiter? Go to Messages → Add Message. Paste the message. Click 🤖 Generate Reply. AI writes a professional reply. Copy it and reply on LinkedIn.
      8
      📲 Set Up Telegram Daily Brief
      1. Message @BotFather on Telegram → /newbot → get your token.
      2. Message your new bot, then visit api.telegram.org/bot<TOKEN>/getUpdates to find your Chat ID.
      3. Add both to backend/.env. Restart server. BRO-BOT sends you a brief every morning at 8 AM IST!
